# Level and new-word hints spec

Status: built for English. How it looks is in [`DESIGN.md`](DESIGN.md#new-word-hints); [`prototype/welcome-level.html`](../prototype/welcome-level.html) and [`prototype/lookup-known.html`](../prototype/lookup-known.html) are where the Word Check and I know this were worked out.

## Goal

While watching, the user sees which word in a line is worth learning now, without a level form or a word list to study. Couplet works out the level from one Word Check and from the words the user saves and knows.

## How it works

1. **Word list:** each learning language with a list has its 50,000 most common everyday words in [`public/frequency`](../public/frequency/README.md), most common first, built by [`scripts/build-frequency.mjs`](../scripts/build-frequency.mjs). The background script serves it to the video page.
2. **Level:** the Word Check stores one size per language in `vocabularyLevels`, such as `en: 3000`.
3. **Band:** hints come from ranks between a start and two and a half times it. The start is the higher of the Word Check and, once 20 words are on review, the rank a quarter of those words sit above ([`hintZone`](../src/lib/hints/hints.ts)).
4. **Line:** each original line hints at most its most common candidate. The 200 most common words, words with an apostrophe, and every word in the Vocabulary, saved or known, are skipped. Without its apostrophe a contraction reads as another listed word: `can't` as `cant`, `I'll` as `ill`. A video hints each word, with simple inflections, once; a line shown again keeps its hint.
5. **Known:** I know this in the Lookup Panel saves the word with its line and Retires it, so it leaves the hints, the marks, and Review.

## Decisions made

| Decision                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Instead of                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Rank in a frequency list measures difficulty; CEFR and JLPT are only rough labels | One open list per language covers every word. Per-word CEFR lists such as Oxford's are copyrighted, and sources disagree on how many words a level means                                                                                                                                                                 | A per-word CEFR or JLPT list                                                                                                                                  |
| wordfreq (CC BY-SA 4.0), filtered with Hunspell, top 50,000                       | It blends subtitles with Wikipedia, news, books, and web text, so science and tech words rank where YouTube uses them, and words since 2018, such as `cryptocurrency`, are in. 50,000 words give C1 and C2 bands words to hint. The dictionary drops names. Share-alike binds the list file and its credit, not our code | FrequencyWords (OpenSubtitles 2018), top 20,000: film dialogue ranked `ubiquitous` past 40,000 and lacked `smartphone`, and the list ended inside the C1 band |
| Tokens are looked up as written, lowercased, without lemmatising                  | A common form is common, so `went` ranks well without becoming `go`. Offline lemmatisers are heavy and per-language                                                                                                                                                                                                      | Reducing every token to its lemma first                                                                                                                       |
| The level comes from saved and known words; the Word Check only starts it         | What the user saves shows where they are now, and it improves as they use Couplet                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Asking for a level and trusting it forever                                                                                                                    |
| The band starts at the higher of the Word Check and the saved words               | Raising the Word Check must take effect even after 20 saves, or the change silently does nothing. Lowering it can't undercut what the saved words show                                                                                                                                                                   | Saved words replacing the Word Check after 20                                                                                                                 |
| No hints until a Word Check or 20 saved words                                     | Guessing a band for a new user marks the wrong words, and a bad first impression gets the feature turned off                                                                                                                                                                                                             | A default band from the first video                                                                                                                           |
| At most one hint a line, once per word a video, never the 200 most common         | Simulated on six English videos, hints came to 4 to 9 a minute, on 11% to 49% of lines. Without the once-per-video rule, repeats made most of the noise                                                                                                                                                                  | Marking every word in the band                                                                                                                                |
| Word Check: rows of six words, pick the last row known                            | One choice instead of judging every word, and the rows show what a level means. Saved words correct anyone who overclaims                                                                                                                                                                                                | Tapping each known word among 27, with made-up words to catch guessing                                                                                        |
| The Word Check opens from a button that names the language                        | The learning language is a guess until the user sets it (`defaultSettings`), so the name shows a wrong guess before any words appear                                                                                                                                                                                     | Showing English words straight away                                                                                                                           |
| A language without a list says so, and gets no hints                              | Each language needs its own list, rows, and checks. Japanese also needs whole-word rows, and kanji words read easily to Chinese speakers                                                                                                                                                                                 | Falling back to English rows                                                                                                                                  |
| No board of recommended words by level or topic                                   | Couplet teaches from the videos the user watches. Decks wait for evidence that users want them                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Topic and level decks like other apps                                                                                                                         |
| I know this saves and Retires, with no new state                                  | Known is already a Stage, and Retired already means off the schedule, so Words, Review, sync, and marks need nothing new. A word's status is none, saved, or known                                                                                                                                                       | A separate known list beside the Vocabulary                                                                                                                   |
| I know this is a text button under the senses                                     | It says what it does, and Known then says what follows. The star stays the one main action                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | A third icon in the panel's top row, which saves height but needs a tooltip                                                                                   |
| No Don't learn                                                                    | Names are filtered out of the list, and I know this hides the rest. A third button would ask a decision on every lookup                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Known, Learn, and Don't learn on every card                                                                                                                   |
| The list ships in the extension and the background script serves it               | It adds about 450 KB per language without growing the content script, and needs no network                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Bundling it into the content script, or downloading it                                                                                                        |
| Hints use dashes; Saved Words keep dots                                           | The two marks read apart by shape, not only colour, and vermilion stays for the selected word                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | A coloured highlight                                                                                                                                          |
