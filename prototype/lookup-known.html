Lookup Panel: I know this

Two versions of the same action: an icon beside save and play, or a text button under the senses. Each scene shows the panel unsaved, saved, and known. Click the buttons: save and known turn each other off.

A · Icon

A circled check joins the row. Filled green means known.

B · Text

"I know this" sits under the senses. Once known, it says what that does.

  • Known saves the word with this line and takes it off the schedule: no Review, no subtitle mark, no new-word hint.
  • Save on a known word puts it back on the schedule. Known on a saved word is the same as Stop reviewing.
  • Undo is the same button again. The Words page lists known words under Known.
