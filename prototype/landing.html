Three steps, inside the video

There's nothing to copy into another app.

  1. Watch

    Open a YouTube video with subtitles. The original and your language appear together.

  2. Click

    Click a word to see its meaning. The video can pause while you read. Press the star to save it.

  3. Review

    Saved words come back when they're due, each with its sentence and a link to that moment in the video.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes. Subtitles, lookups, saving, and review are free. Lookups have a daily limit, which keeps the service free for everyone.

Do I need an account?

Subtitles and word lookups work without one. Sign in with Google when you save your first word.

Which videos work?

YouTube videos with subtitles in the language you're learning, including auto-generated ones. If a video has none, the extension tells you.

Where are my words stored?

In your browser, on this device. Removing the extension removes them too, so export your list first.

What gets sent to other services?

Subtitle lines go to Google Translate. Words you look up go through our server to an AI model. The privacy policy has the details.
